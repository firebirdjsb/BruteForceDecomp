# 0x00155840 remaining tail: live x87 values

Status: authenticated instruction analysis only. The caller remains unmodified,
syntax-failing and behaviorally unverified. No coverage increase is claimed.

The original-image and 1,500-byte caller hashes are checked by
`tools/verify_00155840_original_frame.py`. Eleven additional windows pin the
tail's spill/stack distinctions; the existing upload windows remain checked.

## Required arithmetic boundaries

- 155c1c..155c25 loads `[EBP-1c]`, adds `[EBP-4]`, divides by `[EBP-18]`,
  then spills F32 back to `[EBP-1c]`. RSQRTSS consumes that rounded word and
  MOVSS writes `[EBP-18]`. Its product with `[EBP-4]` stays on the x87 stack
  until the FSTP at 155c4b writes `[EBP-c]`.
- The getter at 155c4e receives EAX=`EBP-38`, ECX=ESI. Its three output words
  must be produced before the following cross-product reads; a disconnected
  zero-argument C call is insufficient.
- At 155c8b the first cross-product X is extended precision. **155c8d is FST,
  not FSTP**: it writes a rounded copy to `[EBP-58]` and retains ST0.
  The norm uses the rounded Y/Z words at `[EBP-54]`/`[EBP-50]`, but squares
  the retained extended X through FLD ST(1) / FMUL ST(2) at 155c9e/155ca0.
  Reloading `[EBP-58]` for that square would change the original calculation.
- 155ca4 spills the squared sum; 155ca7 discards the retained X. RSQRTSS
  at 155ca9 consumes the rounded sum and spills its low result to `[EBP-10]`.
- 155cb3..155cc5 normalizes three reloaded components. Only Z is immediately
  spilled to `[EBP-50]`; normalized X/Y stay extended on the x87 stack.
  The second cross product consumes those retained values and the rounded Z.
  Rounding all three normalized components before that product is unsupported.
- The second basis vector is scaled/stored at 155d07..155d1c. FXCH ST(1)
  at 155d1f then selects the retained normalized X for its final scale/store;
  Y follows, and Z is reloaded from its F32 slot for the last scale/store.

The existing `bf_621c0_x87_operations` cannot express this sequence: it lacks
FDIV m32fp, non-popping FST, register duplication/multiplication/exchange,
FSUBP and a discard pop. The tail also needs an explicit scalar SSE operation
that preserves the specified low-lane approximation and floating environment.
Do not silently implement these with a sequence of ordinary float formulas.

## Matrix and integration boundary

The existing concrete virtual chain verifies a returned guest address for one
guarded table. It does not validate the twelve matrix words at that address,
or establish layouts for all nineteen stored caller-pointer occurrences.
Keep constructor/object-layout evidence separate from arithmetic tests seeded
with synthetic matrices. Integration into `FUN_00155840` remains pending the
complete tail operation and valid matrix data for each supported layout.
