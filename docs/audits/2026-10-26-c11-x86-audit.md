## Source repairs after the audited commit

A follow-up inspection identified three independently checkable source errors:

1. `0x002D29F0`: x86 `MOVSX` at `0x002D2A6C` and `0x002D2A70` loads signed 8-bit dispatch selectors. Ghidra treated both as `float *` and emitted a non-C11 pointer switch. The source now keeps separate signed integer selectors and later real pointers.
2. `0x000AF1A0`: `MOV BYTE [ESP+0x17],DL` at `0x000AF205`, `MOV BYTE [ESP+0x17],1` at `0x000AF359`, and `MOV CL,[ESP+0x17]` at `0x000AF41E` prove the later branch reads `local_101`, not an outgoing argument's high byte.
3. `0x00282720`: the return address after a virtual call (`0x002829B9`) and two `PUSH 1` instructions were falsely reconstructed as whole-array assignments. `LEA EDX,[ESP+0x17]` at `0x002829D3` and `MOV BYTE [ESP+0x1B],AL` at `0x002829DC` instead show a **one-byte output buffer**.

Focused original-XBE instruction assertions were checked locally, and source/Clang checks were added to `.github/workflows/full-c11-inventory.yml` via `scripts/verify_two_guest_byte_aliases.py` and `scripts/verify_00282720_guest_byte_buffer.py`.

**The three source repairs are NOT credited to the reported 238 failures until a subsequent completed full audit specifically covering those commits.** New checks should also be interpreted as operand evidence, not 1:1 whole-function runtime proof.
